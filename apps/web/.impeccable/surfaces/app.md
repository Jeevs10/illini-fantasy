# Surface brief: the league app (every route under apps/web/app)

Mode: Operate. Audience: Illinois basketball staff in a private league, laptop and phone equally. Home leads with the league as a whole. Every screen is rebuilt, the draft room included; behavior, data, and copy are preserved. Navy only, no light theme (user, 2026-10-30). Illinois marks allowed; Block I in the header only.

Pre-season Home leads with the next action and the projected Week 1, not 0.0 vs 0.0.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The league hung like the program's banners in the State Farm Center rafters: every fantasy team is a banner, and the standings are the row of banners overhead. Refuses the category's dark scoreboard dashboard (big VS split, neon accent, card grid).

OWN-WORLD: Deep Illini navy ground (#0C1A33), Illini Blue felt panels (#13294B), steel rules (#2B4166, #5B6B80), banner white ink, Illinois Orange (#FF5F05) only for your team, scores, and primary actions. Graduate (varsity block) only for banner marks and headline numerals; Barlow for interface, Barlow Condensed for caps labels. Tables take one reading size and rank by weight and case. Stitching carries state: solid = actual, dashed = projected, double = final.

STORY: A manager opens Home and sees the whole league overhead, finds their own banner lit, reads this week's matchups with theirs lifted, then acts: add, trade, or follow a game.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead (Block I, nav). Below it the rafter strip: one steel rule across the width, a banner per team hanging in standings order, drop length = exact points-for, monogram and record stitched on. Under it, left: this week's matchups as a list, yours lifted and the rest dimmed a step; right: the season line of weeks and the Needs-you actions. Pre-season the left column leads with the next action and the projected Week 1.

FORM: The Rafters, position 4 of 7 on the grounded list, seed: 74d05661615eee9f. Raises kept: one reading size (acetate manual); focus lifts and the rest dims (title wall); length is an exact quantity (dance score); state by form not hue (emission rail); the season as one line of weeks (transit diagram).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
